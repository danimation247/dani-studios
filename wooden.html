Wooden Box

I've messed up
I've messed up real bad.

Yeah! a mistake not even I can fix,
nor a single soul can change.
So let the truth of the matter be said:

With me dead,
lying in an unforgiving bed,
and my wife and kids
crying from their inner souls,
wishing to bring me back.

Brother, as a comforter,
left to read my tribute,
written by no one else,
but by the one who lies
fast asleep in his bed.

Written for this very moment,
for this very misfortune.

But wait.
It just hit me.
I had neither a wife nor kids.
For the truth of the matter is what left me right here,
fast asleep in the depths of a wooden box.

Behind the Poem

There was really nothing behind this one. Call it a quick wave of inspiration that hit me.
"Could I write something like this again?" Probably not.
